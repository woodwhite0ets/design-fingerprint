## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Use SF Pro (system sans) for everything — never serif
- Use the accent blue #0071e3 ONLY for links and primary buttons, never as surface color
- Keep massive whitespace: sections are separated by at least 96px
- Headlines are huge (Display 56px+) and semibold — they carry the design
- Buttons are pill-shaped (border-radius 980px) with generous padding
- Keep surfaces flat — no shadows, no borders on cards, just background color changes (#f5f5f7 on #ffffff)

**Don't:**
- Don't use shadows or elevation — Apple is flat
- Don't use serif fonts anywhere
- Don't color the background with accent blue — white and #f5f5f7 only
- Don't use uppercase headline styles — Apple headlines are title case
- Don't squeeze content — spacing is part of the design language
- Don't use pure #000 for text on light backgrounds — use #1d1d1f
